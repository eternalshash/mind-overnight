EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: MISSILE INTERCEPTION SIMULATION (PHASES 4 - 6)
================================================================================

1. PHASE 4: 1,000,000-RUN ML BASE & 10-SITE LIVE STRESS TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Develop a high-speed, physics-informed Machine Learning algorithm
  for autonomous missile defense fire control.
- Training Data: Generated 1,000,000 synthetic 3D engagements across 3 threat
  classes (Ballistic, Quasi-Ballistic Weave, Supersonic Cruise) in 1.31 seconds
  (763,246 runs/sec) via parallelized Numba JIT.
- ML Architecture:
  * HistGradientBoostingClassifier for optimal battery dispatch (98.42% accuracy).
  * Multi-target HistGradientBoostingRegressor predicting 3D Intercept Points
    (X, Y, Z), time-to-intercept, and launch elevation/azimuth aiming vectors.
  * Real-time decision latency: < 0.1 ms per incoming threat track.
- Live 10-Site Stress Test: Attacking forces fired 10 simultaneous threats from 10
  distinct launch sites (A0-A9). ML dispatched 10 defender batteries in real-time.
- Results: 9 / 10 Direct Hits (90.0% kill efficiency), average CPA = 4.55 m.


2. PHASE 5: HIGH-DENSITY THEATER DEFENSE (60 MISSILES & 4 WAVES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Scale defense to multi-wave saturation raids with finite battery
  magazines and High-Value Asset (HVA) prioritization.
- Multi-Wave Assault Structure (60 Missiles):
  * Wave 1 (t = 0s): 15 Low-Altitude Supersonic Cruise Missiles (1.5 - 4 km).
  * Wave 2 (t = 25s): 20 Exo-Atmospheric Ballistic Missiles (100 - 135 km).
  * Wave 3 (t = 50s): 15 Quasi-Ballistic Weaving Missiles (30 - 42 km).
  * Wave 4 (t = 75s): 10 Hypersonic Surge Missiles on strategic assets.
- Core Innovations:
  * High-Value Asset (HVA) Protection: Defends 5 strategic installations
    (Command HQ, Airbase, Radar Array, Energy Grid, Logistics Hub).
  * Finite Fleet Magazine Tracking: 10 batteries initialized with 8 missiles each
    (80 total). Automatic handoff to neighboring batteries upon depletion.
  * Layered Shoot-Look-Shoot (BDA): Proximity sensors trigger Tier-2 Terminal
    Interceptor launches for evasive threats surviving primary engagement.
- Results:
  * Primary Tier Direct Hits: 58 / 60 (96.7%).
  * Shoot-Look-Shoot BDA Secondary Kills: 2 / 2 (100% secondary kill rate).
  * Total Theater Survivability: 60 / 60 (100.0% neutralized).
  * Remaining Fleet Inventory: 18 / 80 Interceptors Available.


3. PHASE 6: 1,000-MISSILE MASSIVE SATURATION & SPATIAL VULNERABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Full theater-scale saturation defense across 1,000 missiles in 5
  rounds, analyzing spatial target vulnerability and preventing overfitting.
- Overfitting Prevention: Trained once on a 1,000,000-run physics foundation with
  L2 shrinkage regularization (l2_regularization=1.5), leaf pruning (min_samples_leaf=50),
  and early stopping (98.39% out-of-sample test accuracy).
- 5-Round Randomized Assault Structure (200 Missiles / Round):
  * Round 1 (t = 0s): Low-Altitude Terrain-Following Cruise Saturation.
  * Round 2 (t = 30s): Exo-Atmospheric High-Angle Ballistic Plunge.
  * Round 3 (t = 60s): Quasi-Ballistic Mid-Course Weaving Swarm.
  * Round 4 (t = 90s): Synchronized Mixed Cross-Theater Barrage.
  * Round 5 (t = 120s): Concentrated Strategic HVA Surge.
- Spatial Targeting Vulnerability Findings:
  * Central Sector (Command HQ & Radar Array): Absorbed 45.7% (457 missiles).
  * South Sector (Strategic Airbase & Logistics): Absorbed 38.5% (385 missiles).
  * North Sector (Energy & Power Grid): Absorbed 15.8% (158 missiles).
- Multi-Battery Sector Load Balancing: Neighboring batteries form an integrated
  firing net, sharing radar queues and launch commit delays to balance heavy loads.
- Results:
  * Total Incoming Saturation Threats: 1,000 Missiles across 5 Rounds.
  * Primary Direct Kills: 977 / 1,000 (97.70%), average CPA < 3.5 m.
  * Primary Misses (Evasive High-G Maneuvers): 23 / 1,000 (2.30%).
  * Shoot-Look-Shoot (BDA) Secondary Kills: 23 / 23 (100.0% secondary kill rate).
  * Overall High-Value Asset Survivability: 1,000 / 1,000 (100.00% Defended).
  * Remaining Fleet Magazine Inventory: 177 / 1,200 Interceptors Available.
