3. BREAKDOWN OF WHY IT MAKES SENSE (AEROSPACE & MATHEMATICAL RATIONALE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Why Sub-Timestep CPA Interpolation is Physically Essential:
At closing velocities exceeding Mach 12 (approx. 4,000 m/s), two missiles travel 200 m relative
to each other in a single 0.05 s timestep. If evaluated only at discrete step boundaries, a lethal
intercept occurring at 2 m between steps would appear as a 100 m miss. Continuous quadratic
interpolation computes the exact minimum distance t_cpa in [0, dt], preventing false misses.

2. Why 3D True Proportional Navigation (TPN) with N = 4.5:
TPN commands acceleration normal to the instantaneous line-of-sight (LOS) rate vector:
  a_cmd = N * ||v_I|| * (Omega x r_hat_rel)
A navigation gain N in [3.0, 5.0] is theoretically optimal. Below 3.0, the interceptor is sluggish
against evasive maneuvers; above 5.0, sensor noise causes high-frequency actuator chatter and
aerodynamic energy loss. N = 4.5 balances agility with energy retention.

3. Why Automated Battery Dispatch Minimizes t_go + lambda * delta_Y:
In a 1,000 km theater, deploying a laterally misaligned battery forces the interceptor into high-G
turns, draining kinetic energy. Selecting the battery that minimizes time-to-go and lateral offset
ensures a near-boresight intercept trajectory with maximum velocity reserve for terminal endgame.

4. Why Machine Learning on 3-Second Early Warning Radar Snippets:
In high-density raid environments, standard numerical differential equation solvers take seconds
to converge. A trained Random Forest maps early launch velocity and angular rates to optimal
battery assignment and 3D intercept coordinates in 0.45 ms, enabling automated split-second fire
control dispatch.
